# LATAM compliance landscape — bootstrap digest

Bootstrap digest 2026-09-24 (see seeds/README.md). Descriptive only; certification
status claims must come from the official Huawei Cloud compliance attestations before
pilot.

LGPD is the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, the Brazilian general data protection law.
LGPD applies to personal data processed in Brazil, including cloud workloads hosted in
Brazilian regions such as LA-Sao Paulo1. LFPDPPP is the Ley Federal de Protección de
Datos Personales en Posesión de los Particulares, the Mexican federal personal data
protection law for private parties. LFPDPPP applies to personal data processed in
Mexico, including cloud workloads hosted in Mexican regions such as LA-Mexico City1
and LA-Mexico City2. ISO 27001 is an international information security management
standard commonly required in LATAM public-sector tenders. Huawei Cloud publishes its
compliance certifications and attestations on www.huaweicloud.com.
