# structure-axis Specification

## Purpose
The product's declared shape outranks a shared marketing word.

## Requirements

### Requirement: the structure axis separates shapes

When the query carries at least two distinct data-display markers,
the scorer SHALL credit candidates whose scenario or category names
the data-board shape (operation, live-artifacts, analytics,
monitoring) 6xidf per distinct marker up to three; briefs with fewer
than two markers SHALL score exactly as before.

#### Scenario: nominal

- **WHEN one brief reads "marketing site with odds table and live
  scores" and another reads "marketing landing page for a tourism
  site"**
- **THEN the first SHALL prefer the data-board candidate and the
  second the landing candidate**
